The House

A palace of thresholds, built in the language of Tlemcen

Tlemcen is a city of carved gateways. The Alcazar is built in the same spirit: pointed windows, lobed arches, and galleries that lead you from one space to the next.

Its name means "the castle", and it sounds like Al Karaz, the cherry the region is known for. The meeting rooms are named after the city's old names: Mechouar, Altava, Pomaria and Setti. The hotel opened in 2011 and was relaunched as Alcazar Tlemcen in 2026, managed by SIH.
