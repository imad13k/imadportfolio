Data, statement, proof.Data, communiqué, preuve.

In Arabic, Beyane (بيان) means data, statement and proof. It is how we work: every idea starts from facts, becomes a clear message, and is judged by its results.En arabe, Beyane (بيان) signifie data, communiqué et preuve.
