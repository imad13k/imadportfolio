Who we are

A family project with a national mission.

Founded by brothers Allel and Seddik Amry in the Hassi Ameur industrial zone near Oran, Orion Lab was built around one conviction: patients in Algeria should not have to wait on imports for cancer treatment.

The company brings together high-containment oral solid production, aseptic fill-finish for injectables and an in-house quality control laboratory under one roof.
